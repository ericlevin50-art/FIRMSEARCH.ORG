FirmSearch Research Standards

FirmSearch is intended for legitimate, lawful research and information-gathering purposes.

  • No requests involving illegal activity.
  • No hacking, unauthorized access, or cyber abuse.
  • No fraud, scams, or deception.
  • No harassment, stalking, or invasion of privacy.
  • No explicit sexual or exploitative material.
  • No requests intended to harm another person.
  • No other unlawful, dangerous, or seriously unethical requests.

FirmSearch reserves the right to decline any request that appears unlawful, unsafe, abusive, or inappropriate.
