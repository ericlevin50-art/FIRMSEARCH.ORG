Human-Powered Research

Real research. Real replies. Real people.

Research Standards

FirmSearch.org is a human-powered research service. Our goal is to help people find useful information, sources, records, and other publicly available material on the web.

How We Research

Research requests are reviewed and searched by a real person. We use publicly available information and sources that we believe may be relevant to the request.

Accuracy

We make a reasonable effort to provide useful and accurate information, but research results can contain errors, outdated information, or incomplete information. Sources should be independently verified before being relied upon for important decisions.

What We Will Not Search For

We will not knowingly conduct searches intended to facilitate illegal activity, harassment, abuse, invasion of privacy, exploitation, or other harmful or inappropriate activity.

We may decline or limit a request when we believe that conducting the search would create a reasonable risk of harm or misuse.

Public Information

Our research is generally limited to information that is publicly accessible. We do not promise access to private, confidential, restricted, or protected information.

No Guarantee

A research request does not guarantee that useful results will be found. Some subjects simply may not have enough reliable information publicly available.

Our Goal

FirmSearch.org exists to provide straightforward, human-assisted research without requiring an account or a paywall.
